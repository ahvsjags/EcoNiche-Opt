## Endpoint Definitions

- primary_recist: CR/PR/MR/R/DCB vs SD/PD/NR/NDB; this is the conservative primary endpoint.
- clinical_benefit: CR/PR/MR/SD/R/DCB vs PD/NR/NDB.
